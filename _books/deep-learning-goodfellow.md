The canonical textbook on deep learning. Covers the theoretical underpinnings of neural networks from first principles through regularisation, optimisation, convolutional networks, sequence models, and generative models. Despite its age relative to transformer-era developments, the foundations it builds are indispensable — reading this alongside active research papers makes both significantly more tractable. A book you return to repeatedly rather than read once and shelve.
