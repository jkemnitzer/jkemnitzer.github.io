A thorough survey of prompting strategies — from basic zero/few-shot techniques through chain-of-thought, retrieval augmentation, and agent-style orchestration. Practical and well-structured. From a security research standpoint it is also a useful catalogue of the attack surface: every prompting pattern that grants the model more autonomy or instruction-following latitude is simultaneously a potential injection vector. Worth reading with that lens in mind.
