Huyen's follow-up to Designing Machine Learning Systems, updated for the foundation model era. Covers the full stack of building AI applications on top of large models: evaluation, retrieval-augmented generation, agents, latency and cost trade-offs, and the organisational challenges of shipping AI products. Grounded, pragmatic, and honest about what remains unsolved. A timely read as the gap between research models and production deployments continues to define the field.
