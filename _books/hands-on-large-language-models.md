A visually rich and accessible deep-dive into how LLMs process and generate language. Alammar's signature illustration-heavy style makes complex concepts like attention, tokenisation, and embedding spaces genuinely intuitive. Covers text classification, semantic search, fine-tuning, and multimodal models with practical code throughout. Less focused on the theoretical depth of Raschka's book, but an excellent complement — especially for building intuition around embedding space behaviour.
