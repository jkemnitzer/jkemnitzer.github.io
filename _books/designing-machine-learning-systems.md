Fills the gap between ML research and production reality. Huyen addresses data engineering, training pipelines, deployment strategies, monitoring, and the organisational dynamics that determine whether an ML system actually works in the wild. Particularly valuable for understanding the hidden complexity in deploying models to SMEs — the exact context I operate in daily. One of the few ML books written by someone who has clearly shipped real systems under real constraints.
