A rigorous consolidation of the mathematical foundations underlying modern machine learning — linear algebra, calculus, probability, and optimisation — presented with enough depth to actually understand why algorithms work, not just how to apply them. Dense in places, but an honest reference that earns its keep on the shelf. Essential reading when starting a research position that demands you go beyond calling library functions.
