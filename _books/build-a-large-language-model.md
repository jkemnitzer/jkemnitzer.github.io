Exactly what it promises: a ground-up implementation of an LLM in PyTorch, from tokenisation through attention mechanisms, pretraining, and instruction fine-tuning. Raschka's pedagogical clarity is exceptional — the code is clean, the explanations are honest about trade-offs, and the progression is genuinely instructive rather than just demonstrative. For anyone working on transformer internals or mechanistic interpretability, building the thing from scratch is the right way to understand it.
