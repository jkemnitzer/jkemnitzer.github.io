A concise, practical guide to making LLMs reliable in production contexts — covering prompt engineering, retrieval-augmented generation, fine-tuning workflows, and evaluation strategies. Lighter in theoretical depth than the LLM Engineer's Handbook but quicker to read and well-focused on the key failure modes practitioners encounter. A useful reference for the reliability and robustness side of deployment.
